# Luxury Minimal

## Intent

Create a premium, quiet, high-end poster using restraint, whitespace, refined typography, tactile materials, and elegant image direction.

## Typography

- Use refined serif, elegant sans-serif, or subtle serif/sans contrast.
- Use generous tracking and careful line spacing.
- Keep headline short, calm, and spacious.
- Metadata should feel like a luxury label, not a flyer block.

## Palette

Muted neutrals: ivory, warm gray, black, espresso, stone, sand, champagne. Accent with gold, deep green, burgundy, or muted blue.

## Imagery

Use premium photography, macro detail, sculptural object, elegant portrait, or restrained abstract material.

## Texture / Material

Matte paper, subtle grain, embossed feel, foil hint, stone, silk, glass, shadow, natural light.

## Composition Rules

- Use large negative space.
- Place subject with confidence; do not over-explain.
- Make every element feel intentional and expensive.
- Prefer one strong image and very few words.

## Avoid

Loud colors, busy backgrounds, cheap gold effects, overdecorated frames, generic luxury clichés, and dense marketing copy.
