# Organic Craft

## Intent

Create a warm, tactile, human poster with handmade texture, natural palette, soft imperfection, and approachable charm.

## Typography

- Use friendly serif, soft sans-serif, or carefully controlled hand-lettered direction.
- Keep handmade type readable and not overly whimsical.
- Use small labels like tags, stamps, or notes.

## Palette

Earthy and warm: cream, kraft, olive, terracotta, clay, sage, cocoa, muted mustard, dusty rose.

## Imagery

Use hand-drawn marks, cut paper, botanical forms, product ingredients, soft illustration, scanned objects, or natural textures.

## Texture / Material

Paper grain, risograph texture, pencil/ink marks, fabric, cardboard, stamp, tape, organic edges.

## Composition Rules

- Use slight irregularity while preserving alignment.
- Let materials and texture create warmth.
- Keep focal point simple and friendly.

## Avoid

Messy scrapbook clutter, childish doodles, unreadable hand lettering, overly cute icons, and synthetic glossy effects.
