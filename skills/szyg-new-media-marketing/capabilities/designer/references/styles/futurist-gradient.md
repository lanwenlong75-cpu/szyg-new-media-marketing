# Futurist Gradient

## Intent

Create a premium technology poster with sophisticated gradients, glass/ light effects, abstract depth, and forward-looking polish.

## Typography

- Use modern geometric or neo-grotesk sans-serif direction.
- Keep type crisp, minimal, and high-contrast.
- Avoid overly sci-fi fonts; let composition feel advanced rather than gimmicky.

## Palette

Dark or light tech base with mesh gradients: indigo, cyan, violet, electric blue, magenta, soft green, silver, graphite.

## Imagery

Use abstract 3D forms, luminous surfaces, glassmorphism, orbital geometry, data-like particles, or premium product/interface abstraction.

## Texture / Material

Mesh gradient, glass, glow, blur, metallic sheen, soft depth, controlled grain.

## Composition Rules

- Use one luminous focal field or abstract object.
- Keep text in calm high-contrast zones.
- Use glow as accent, not decoration everywhere.
- Preserve a premium sense of space and depth.

## Avoid

Cyberpunk clutter, excessive neon, fake UI fragments, random circuits, unreadable glow text, and generic AI orb clichés.
