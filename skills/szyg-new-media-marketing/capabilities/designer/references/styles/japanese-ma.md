# Japanese Ma

## Intent

Create a quiet, refined poster using Japanese-inspired negative space, subtle asymmetry, natural materials, and poetic restraint.

## Typography

- Use elegant Mincho-style serif, restrained sans-serif, or vertical text direction where appropriate.
- Keep copy sparse and calm.
- Use small metadata with precise placement.
- Avoid decorative faux-Asian type.

## Palette

Warm white, sumi black, stone gray, indigo, moss, clay, muted red seal accent, natural paper tones.

## Imagery

Single branch, ceramic vessel, shadow, fabric fold, architectural detail, ink mark, empty room, or seasonal motif.

## Texture / Material

Washi paper, ink wash, natural fibers, soft grain, gentle shadow, hand-pressed imperfection.

## Composition Rules

- Use emptiness as the main design element.
- Place focal subject off-center.
- Use asymmetry and quiet scale contrast.
- Let the poster breathe; do not fill space.

## Avoid

Cliché Japanese symbols, fake calligraphy, crowded zen decoration, red sun clichés, and tourist-poster stereotypes.
