# Cinematic Key Art

## Intent

Create a dramatic poster with film-poster polish, atmospheric lighting, strong hero subject, and premium title lockup.

## Typography

- Use cinematic title typography with strong spacing and controlled effects.
- Keep credits/details small but legible in a footer or lower lockup.
- Avoid excessive font variety.

## Palette

Moody cinematic palettes: deep black, midnight blue, amber, teal, crimson, silver, fog gray, warm spotlight tones.

## Imagery

Use a hero person/object/scene, dramatic lighting, depth, atmosphere, silhouettes, rim light, cinematic environment.

## Texture / Material

Film grain, haze, light bloom, subtle vignette, depth-of-field, realistic shadows.

## Composition Rules

- Establish one dramatic focal subject.
- Place title lockup in a classic poster zone.
- Use atmospheric negative space for text.
- Make lighting guide the eye.

## Avoid

Overcrowded movie-poster clichés, random characters, fake actor credits unless provided, unreadable title effects, and excessive lens flare.
