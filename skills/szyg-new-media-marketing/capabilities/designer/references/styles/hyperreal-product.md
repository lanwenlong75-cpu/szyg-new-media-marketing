# Hyperreal Product

## Intent

Create a high-conversion product poster using hyperreal product rendering, premium lighting, material detail, and clear commercial hierarchy.

## Typography

- Use clean commercial sans-serif typography.
- Keep the product name and supplied benefits direct. Include action copy only when explicitly provided in the source.
- Use callouts sparingly and align them with product geometry.

## Palette

Product-led palette with clean contrast: white, black, silver, brand accent, soft gradient, studio neutral.

## Imagery

Large hyperreal product hero, macro material detail, condensation, texture, reflections, exploded feature detail, controlled props.

## Texture / Material

Studio lighting, glossy highlight, soft shadow, realistic material, liquid/metal/glass/plastic accuracy.

## Composition Rules

- Product is the main hero and must look accurate and desirable.
- Use benefit callouts only where they support conversion.
- Keep background premium and uncluttered.
- When action copy is supplied, integrate it as a plain typographic footer or caption without button styling.

## Avoid

Distorted packaging, wrong labels, fake logos, too many callouts, messy props, and unrealistic product scale.
